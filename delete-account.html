Delete your Daily Tracker account

You can ask for your Daily Tracker account, and the personal data held with it, to be deleted at any time. This applies to accounts used on the website and in the Daily Tracker mobile app.

How to ask

  1. 1Write to help@dailytracker.lol from the email address your Daily Tracker account uses, with the subject "Delete my Daily Tracker account".
  2. 2We reply to that address to confirm the request really is yours.
  3. 3Once you confirm, the account is deleted within 30 days and we tell you when it is done.
Email help@dailytracker.lol

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile: name, handle, photo, cover image, headline, bio, location and links
  • Your posts, published records, comments, endorsements, follows and saved items
  • Your notification settings and the push tokens of your devices
  • Your membership of every workspace

What is kept, and for how long

Work logged inside a workspace
It is part of that workspace’s record. It stays with the workspace but is no longer linked to you.
Messages you sent
They stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from a deleted account.
Backups
Deleted information can remain in backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
What the law requires
Anything we are legally obliged to keep, for as long as that obligation lasts.

Deleting some data without closing the account

You can delete your own posts, comments and messages in the product, and change or clear your profile in Settings. To have anything else removed while keeping your account, write to the same address and say what you want deleted.
